📥 1. 我们收集哪些信息1. Information We Collect

我们在您使用 AI Open Fortune 服务时，可能收集以下类型的信息：

您主动提供的信息：

  • 账户信息：姓名、电子邮箱地址、密码（加密存储）
  • 市场偏好：您选择的市场版本（A股版或全球版）
  • 支付信息：订阅套餐选择、支付记录（不存储完整银行卡信息）
  • 联系信息：您通过邮件或客服渠道与我们沟通的内容

自动收集的信息：

  • 设备信息：浏览器类型、操作系统、设备标识符
  • 日志信息：IP 地址、访问时间、页面浏览记录
  • 使用数据：功能使用频率、点击行为（匿名化处理）

We may collect the following types of information when you use AI Open Fortune services:

Information You Provide:

  • Account information: name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Market preference: your selected market version (A-Share or Global)
  • Payment information: subscription plan, payment records (no full card numbers stored)
  • Communications: content of emails or support messages you send us

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device information: browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Log data: IP address, access time, page views
  • Usage data: feature usage frequency, click behavior (anonymized)

⚙️ 2. 如何使用您的信息2. How We Use Your Information

我们使用收集的信息用于以下目的：

  • 提供、维护和改善我们的 AI 投资策略服务
  • 发送账户验证邮件、订阅确认邮件和每周投资周报

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our AI investment strategy services
  • Send account verification emails, subscription confirmations, and weekly reports
  • Process your subscription payments and refund requests
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send important service change notifications
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats

✅ We will not use your personal information for unrelated commercial promotions or sell your data to third-party advertisers.

🛡️ 3. 如何保护您的信息3. How We Protect Your Information

我们采用行业标准的安全措施保护您的个人信息：

  • 传输加密：全站启用 HTTPS/TLS 加密传输
  • 密码安全：使用 bcryptjs 算法加盐哈希，从不明文存储密码
  • 访问控制：JWT Token 身份验证，API 接口限流保护
  • 最小权限：内部员工仅能访问工作所需的最少信息
  • 定期审计：定期对系统安全性进行审查和更新

⚠️ 请注意：互联网传输不能保证100%安全。如果您怀疑账户安全受损，请立即联系我们。

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: Full HTTPS/TLS encryption across the entire site
  • Password Security: bcryptjs algorithm with salting, passwords never stored in plaintext
  • Access Control: JWT Token authentication, API rate limiting protection
  • Least Privilege: Internal staff only access the minimum information required for their work
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews and updates

⚠️ Please note: Internet transmission cannot be guaranteed 100% secure. If you suspect your account security has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

🤝 4. 信息共享与披露4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

我们不会出售、出租或交易您的个人信息。仅在以下有限情况下共享：

  • 服务提供商：与帮助我们运营服务的可信第三方（如邮件服务 Resend、支付处理方），这些方受保密协议约束
  • 法律要求：当法律法规、法院命令或政府机构要求时
  • 安全保护：为防止欺诈、保护用户安全或调查违规行为时
  • 业务转让：公司合并或收购时，您的信息可能作为资产转移，届时我们会提前通知您

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate our services (e.g., email service Resend, payment processors), bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government agency
  • Security Protection: To prevent fraud, protect user safety, or investigate violations
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as an asset; we will notify you in advance

🍪 5. Cookie 使用说明5. Cookie Policy

我们使用以下类型的本地存储技术：

  • 必要性 Cookie：用于保持登录状态（JWT Token），这是服务正常运行所必须的
  • 偏好存储：记住您的语言偏好（localStorage）
  • 分析 Cookie：匿名统计页面访问量，帮助我们改善用户体验

您可以通过浏览器设置管理或删除 Cookie，但这可能影响某些功能的正常使用。

We use the following types of local storage technologies:

  • Necessary Cookies: Used to maintain login state (JWT Token), essential for service operation
  • Preference Storage: Remember your language preference (localStorage)
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymous page view statistics to help us improve user experience

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect certain features.

✅ 6. 您的权利6. Your Rights

根据适用法律，您享有以下权利：

  • 访问权：查看我们持有的您的个人信息
  • 更正权：要求更正不准确的个人信息
  • 删除权：要求删除您的账户和相关数据（"被遗忘权"）
  • 数据可携权：以结构化格式获取您的数据副本
  • 反对权：反对某些类型的数据处理
  • 退订权：随时退订营销邮件（邮件底部有退订链接）

如需行使上述权利，请发送邮件至 privacy@aiopenfortune.com，我们将在30天内回复。

Under applicable law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: View the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and related data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive a copy of your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Unsubscribe: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time (unsubscribe link in email footer)

To exercise these rights, please email privacy@aiopenfortune.com. We will respond within 30 days.

🗂️ 7. 数据保留期限7. Data Retention

  • 账户数据：在您注销账户后，我们将在30天内删除您的个人信息
  • 交易记录：根据法律要求，财务记录保留7年
  • 日志数据：服务器日志保留90天后自动删除

  • Account Data: We will delete your personal information within 30 days after account cancellation
  • Transaction Records: Financial records are retained for 7 years as required by law
  • Log Data: Server logs are automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Email Communications: Support emails are retained for 2 years for dispute resolution

👶 8. 未成年人保护8. Children's Privacy

AI Open Fortune 的投资服务面向18岁及以上的成年人。我们不会故意收集未成年人的个人信息。如果您发现未成年人注册了我们的服务，请立即联系我们，我们将删除相关账户。

AI Open Fortune's investment services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you discover that a minor has registered for our services, please contact us immediately and we will delete the relevant account.
